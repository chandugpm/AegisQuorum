Glossary · 8 plain-English definitions
SHA-256
A cryptographic fingerprint function. Change one bit of data and the fingerprint changes completely — that is how tampering is detected.
Ed25519
The digital-signature algorithm nodes use to sign audit entries, proving who wrote them and that they were not altered.
Quorum
No single actor can change policy. At least 2 of 3 independent human authorities must approve with passkeys.
Quarantine
Cutting a compromised node off from the cluster so it cannot affect healthy peers.
Honeypot
A decoy that pretends to be the infected node. The attacker interacts with a safe fake while defenses watch.
Antibody / inoculation
A signed description of the attack, gossiped to healthy nodes so they become immune before the attack reaches them.
MTTD / MTTR
Mean time to detect / mean time to recover — how many milliseconds pass between the attack starting, being noticed, and being fully healed.
SIR model
Epidemiology math (Susceptible → Infected → Recovered) used to predict how fast the attack would spread without defense.
